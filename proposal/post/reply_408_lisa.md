@lisa-bengtsson Agreed, and I think the simplest form needs no flag: delete the early returns. A return is only safe if nothing after it still has work for a quiet column, and CA, progomega (the memory, and `dt_decay` in #403), coldpool and MG's `qlcn`/`qicn` all have some. Without them SAMF gives the same answers for any blocking by construction; #403 as posted doesn't with coldpool alone, progomega alone, or CA with MYNN (standalone test, blocks of 1–6 columns).

The cost is small. In `control_p8` on Hercules (3 runs each), `GFS Physics` time goes from 37.2 to 37.7 s (+1.3 %), within the run-to-run noise of the total, and answers are bit-identical. If that's too much, a per-column `pending(i)` with one exit keeps the returns where they're safe, and also passes the blocking test. Should I add the deletion here, or send it to #403?
