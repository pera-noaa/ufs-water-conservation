For H(x) the OOPS option does the same job, but its TL and adjoint are still nearest-state: `GetValues` widens the window only in the nonlinear path ([L395](https://github.com/JCSDA/oops/blob/ef934f23f10187653a7cb7e9024dec9bca29716c/src/oops/base/GetValues.h#L395)), while `processTL` and `processAD` keep `halfWidth_ = tstep/2` ([L628](https://github.com/JCSDA/oops/blob/ef934f23f10187653a7cb7e9024dec9bca29716c/src/oops/base/GetValues.h#L628), [L805](https://github.com/JCSDA/oops/blob/ef934f23f10187653a7cb7e9024dec9bca29716c/src/oops/base/GetValues.h#L805)). So a 4D-Var with `time interpolation: linear` pairs an interpolated H(x) with a nearest-state TL and adjoint; `TimeOperLinInterp` is consistent in all three. Once the OOPS TL/AD interpolate, I'd be glad to retire it.
