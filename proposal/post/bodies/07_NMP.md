Companion of ufs-community/ccpp-physics#TBD, which steps Noah-MP land once per time step through a new optional `iter` argument of `noahmpdrv_run`. In atmosphere–land builds the land component is compiled against this copy's interface but calls the ccpp-physics routine; without `iter` declared here, `control_p8_atmlnd` crashes. Declares the same optional `iter` in `drivers/ccpp/noahmpdrv.F90` and `.meta`. Tested in ufs-community/ufs-weather-model#TBD (`atml` build, land-component tests).
