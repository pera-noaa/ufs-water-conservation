Thanks @JongilHan66. I think two things are mixed here. Sigma surfaces make the diffusion less accurate over steep terrain, but not non-conservative. The leak is from applying the fluxes to mixing ratios: a flux changes two columns' `q` by equal and opposite amounts, but their air mass (`delp`) differs, so what one loses the other does not gain. On the terrain-following levels that happens wherever surface pressure varies, and it would on z surfaces too. The fix stays on sigma surfaces: diffuse `q·delp` at the faces and divide each cell's net flux by its own `delp`. Making it optional is fine for us; if you keep it on, we can test this form.
