## Description:
14 bug fixes that remove water, energy and tracer-mass errors in SAMF, the PBL, Noah-MP, GFDL MP v3 and the SAMF single-precision build. Each fix is one commit in its subcomponent; the ccpp-physics PRs give the measured effects. Largest effects: SAMF drops the heat of fusion of the ice it detrains (0.42 W/m²); GFDL MP v3 creates 1.03 % of its precipitation; `control_p8_mynn` depends on the domain decomposition. In the global P8 tests, mean 3-D temperature rises 0.001–0.006 K, from the restored heat of fusion, and precipitation changes by −0.27 to +0.29 %. `hafs_global_1nest_atm` has a pre-existing SAMF instability (ufs-community/ccpp-physics#401); with these fixes 0 of 20 ensemble members crash, as on develop. One of the staged WM PRs for #3404.
