## Pre-registration (written 2026-10-29 16:01 MDT, before any build or run)

Arms (C96L127, 24 h, dt 720, 64-bit dycore, `FV3_GFS_v17_p8_ugwpv1`, six passive tracers and meters as trsamf/trred):
- **A** = package 1: ccpp-physics f2a09680 (`pr1-sa1x` = ufs/dev 9b65688c + #405 + #408 + #414), tree copied from
  `$U/pr1/rt_sxs`. **B** = A + `27_T1_02b.diff`. Same tree, same instrumentation; only the two SAMF files differ.
- Members p0, p3, p4, p5 (trred's IC, T × (1 + 1e-7 N(0,1)), seeds 3/4/5) in both arms. Null arm **N** = exe A on
  four other perturbations (p1, p2 from `cons/tracer`, new p6, p7).

Estimators (fixed now; all also applied to the old trsamf/trred runs to show they reproduce the old numbers):
- E1 pile-up: pt_umoist min/max (× 1e-6) at 24 h from the `TU` meter line; pt_cnv and pt_plume max/global-mean at 24 h.
- E2 o3 / TKE: `trred/scripts/item346.py` item 3 on 1000 rain-producing deep columns dumped from A0 (`TRRED_DUMP=40`):
  median over C > 2 columns of rms(incr nsub=1 − incr sub-stepped)/rms(incr sub-stepped) for o3; share of C > 2
  columns with negative TKE, nsub=1 vs sub-stepped. Also the share of dumped columns with C > 2 (old dD0 vs A0).
- E3 mass: SAMF deep and shallow mass terms (TP slots 1, 2), 24 h sum / initial mass, max |.| over tracers, arm B.
- E4 forecast: `trred/scripts/noise.py` B vs A (members 0,3,4,5): zonal cells (18 lat × 4 bands) with |paired t| > 3.18
  for T, q, o3 (3.6 of 72 expected); max zonal |mean d| for T; o3 global-mean relative change.

Pass rule for "R2-1's text keeps its numbers": every headline number within ±25 % (relative) of the old one, counts
within ±3 cells. Any number outside → text gets the new number. If C > 2 columns fall below 1 % of dumped deep columns
in A, the issue's premise needs rewriting, not just new numbers.

Gates (each with a known-bad):
- G-src: A's SAMF files = git blobs of f2a09680 before instrumentation; B = A + 02b exactly (re-derived by patching
  the pristine file and instrumenting it). Known-bad: `patch --dry-run -R` of 02b on A fails.
- G-exe: `samfdeep_trsub`/`samfshal_trsub` symbols in exe B, absent from exe A.
- G-rep: `trred/scripts/gate.py` G2 on the A0 dump: replay of the package-1 tracer operator reproduces the model's o3
  increment to ≤ 1e-12 of it; its flipped-start known-bad must fail.
- G-null: E4's counting on N vs A must give a count consistent with chance (≤ 8 of 72); positive control: o3 detected
  in B vs A.
- G-est: E2 on the old dD0 dump reproduces 0.19 / 0.13 / 0.016; E4 on old nA*/nB* reproduces 9 / 11 / 13.
