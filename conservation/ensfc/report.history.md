## 1. Frames

- **Land (Noah-MP)** heat content H:
  - sensible: Noah-MP's own heat capacities × (T − 273.16 K), for soil, snow layers and glacier ice layers;
  - latent: −hfus per kg of ice (snow layers, layerless snow, soil ice, canopy ice);
  - the canopy is massless, i.e. it has no sensible term.
- **Sea ice:** the Winton heat content of ice3lay, with liquid at tfi = −0.054 C as reference; snow is −li per kg.
- **Open water:** an open boundary. NSST is a diagnostic skin and warm layer on top of a prescribed Tref.
- **Hand-off:** each component's booked flux, weighted by type fraction (txl, txi, txo from frac_grid), against what the atmosphere books for the cell:
  - SW: xmu × radiation-time surface net;
  - LW: radiation-time surface net;
  - SH, LH: dtsfc1 and dqsfc1 as applied by the PBL;
  - frozen precipitation: from the MP, previous step.

## 3. Open-ocean boundary (prescribed SST)

F_ocn is defined from the atmosphere side, for the open-water fraction of each cell:

F_ocn = adjsfcnsw_cell(open-water share) + emis·adjsfcdlw − emis·σ·T_skin⁴ − SH − LH − hfus·P_frozen,ocean

- In icy cells, SH and LH are sfc_sice's lead fluxes (they are what the PBL applies), and the open-water SW share is wfrac·sfcnsw − txi·sneti = txo·snetw. That is exactly sfc_sice's own partition, so SI3 is not needed: it was a definition, not a defect.
- NSST's f_nsol, qrain and warm-layer resets are internal to the diagnostic. They change the skin T, hence the fluxes, but not the boundary definition.
- Value (mx3): SW 134.48 − LW 39.68 − SH 7.86 − LH 79.67 − frozen precipitation 0.62 ≈ +6.6 W m-2 into the prescribed ocean.

## 6. Precipitation phase at the hand-off

Where each part decides the frozen/liquid split:
- **Atmosphere:** Thompson books its species frozen. SAMF convective precipitation leaves as liquid, with no ice phase (D3a).
- **Host srflag (Thompson branch, `GFS_MP_generic_post.F90` 466–483):** convective precipitation counts as snow when the surface composite tsfc < 273.15.
- **Sea ice:** uses srflag (consistent with the host).
- **Noah-MP soil (NM10):** convective precipitation is snow when T1 < 273.16 (sfctmp). NM10 says it follows "the host's own rule (L495–516)", but that is the MG branch, which uses t850. The Thompson rule is tsfc.
- **Noah-MP glacier:** a T1 rule for all precipitation, ignoring the MP species.

Measured:
- The surfaces take hfus × 0.043 W m-2 more frozen precipitation than the atmosphere released as frozen. Land received 0.4281 + 0.0552 against MP 0.4455; ice 0.1426 against 0.1375.
- Host-classified frozen convective precipitation: 2.18e-7 kg m-2 s-1, i.e. hfus × 0.0726 W m-2 globally, ocean included.

**Which side should change.** Physically the atmosphere should pay. Convective precipitation reaching a sub-freezing surface froze aloft, and the defect is that SAMF has no ice-phase precipitation. Making the surface accept liquid would put rain on −20 °C snow.

Fixes:
- **PH1:** the lowest layer is heated by hfus·ρw·csnow/(cp·m_p), using the physics mass as in CP1.
- **PH2:** Noah-MP takes the host's convective decision, derived from srflag. This makes NM10 do what its text says.
- **PH3:** the glacier uses srflag.

Closure: frozen precipitation received equals the host's srflag·P exactly — land soil 1.271902e-6 = 1.271902e-6, glacier and ice exact. The atmosphere releases MP frozen + PH1-paid convective = srflag·P in each column.
Impact: global T mean +5e-4 K, rms 0.152; ice SH mean −0.14 W m-2 (noise −0.006); everything else is noise. Class: PHYSICS (missing term).

Coordination:
- The energy agent's MP-segment flux F must add hfus·csnow.
- If SAMF gets ice-phase precipitation (D3a, encpl S1), PH1 must move into SAMF so the payment is not counted twice.

## 7. Water enthalpy across the surface in encpl's frame F (PAH and evaporation)

Frame F: water crosses at T1 and φ_s. Evaporation brings h_v(T1) + φ_s = Lv00 + cp_v·T1 + φ_s; precipitation takes
h_w(T1) + φ_s. The surfaces now supply per column and step (M0, meters `instrument_es_sfc7.py`, esb(134:140)):

**Evaporation.** E for each surface type, the evaporating temperature T_s, and T1:
- land: canopy evaporation and transpiration at tv, ground at tg; glacier at tg;
- sea ice: at tice;
- open water: at the final tskin.

| type | ΣE(T_s − T0) | ΣE(T1 − T0) | Kirchhoff (c_l − cp_v)·E(T_s − T0) | vapour skin→T1, cp_v·E(T_s − T1) | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| land (closed) | 9.540e-5 | 7.585e-5 | +0.223 | +0.036 | **+0.259** |
| sea ice | −1.15e-7 | −1.98e-7 | −0.0003 | +0.0002 | ≈0 |
| open water (open) | 7.204e-4 | 6.610e-4 | +1.685 | +0.110 | **+1.795** |

- Units: the two temperature columns are kg m-2 s-1 K globally; the other columns are W m-2.
- E-weighted T_s − T1: 2.2 K overall (land 5.5 K, water 1.9 K), against encpl's 2.07 K. The total, 2.05, compares with encpl's −1.90.
- **Latent heat removed per kg:**
  - land: 2.528e6 J/kg (hvap, plus hsub on the frozen share; NM8 host constants; CMC makes charged = carried);
  - sea ice: hvap for sublimation, with no ice mass removed. This is a water leak; frame F needs L_s(T) and the mass taken from the ice;
  - NSST: hvap.
- **What it means.**
  - About 88 % of the evaporation term is the prescribed ocean. For an open boundary it is **booking only**: F_ocn supplies h_v(T1)·E, so the ocean takes the 1.80 W m-2 by definition.
  - For the closed land the 0.26 W m-2 is real. Closing it is a **model change**, either:
    - (a) Noah-MP charges L(T_s) = hvap + (cp_v − c_l)(T_s − T0) (hsub analogue for ice; ≈ 2.5 % of land LH), and the coupling adds cp_v·(T_s − T1)·E to the surface sensible flux; or
    - (b) the coupling heats the lowest layer by E·[hvap + c_l·T_s − h_v(T1)], i.e. encpl's bit 4 evaluated at the exported T_s instead of T1.
  - Option (b) keeps the surface physics unchanged.

**Precipitation.** Noah-MP lands precipitation at T1 (sfctmp) and books PAH = c·(T1 − T_v or T_g)·P into the canopy/ground balances.
- Land frame F input c_l·P·(T1 − T0) = 0.485 W m-2 global.
- PAH is −0.0134. In the E_P frame nobody pays it; in frame F it is consistent if:
  - PAH uses c_l (4185.5) and c_i (1972) instead of Noah-MP's 4188 and 2094;
  - the ±20 W m-2 clip is removed (5e-5 W m-2);
  - snow stored in layers uses the same c_i as F (Noah-MP's 2283.6). This is the −0.010 "snowfall stored cold" of §5.
- Sea ice: snowfall lands with no sensible heat and needs c_i·(T1 − T_ref)·P_snow booked. Rain on ice passes to the ocean.
- Ocean: open; NSST's qrain is internal.
- **Booking vs model change.** With those Noah-MP constants changed (a few lines; a tiny model change), precipitation becomes booking only.

**Runoff and aquifer exchange (§9) must be listed in F as open-boundary exports at their layer T.** In an absolute-T frame
runoff alone carries c_w·T·runoff ≈ 0.6 W m-2.

## 8. SW and LW hand-off

**SW, decomposition for soil columns** (M0; A, B and C are the absorbed SW under three assumptions):

| quantity | W m-2 global |
|---|---|
| A: radiation's land share (radiation-time Noah-MP albedos × the radiation's 4 band fluxes) | 39.337 |
| B: this step's Noah-MP albedos × the same band fluxes | 39.164 |
| C: Noah-MP fsa | 39.181 |

- **Real land interface error:** C − A = −0.156. Of this:
  - albedo change since the hourly radiation call (cosz and snow): B − A = −0.173;
  - Noah-MP's own 0.7/0.3 direct/diffuse and 50/50 VIS/NIR split: C − B = +0.016;
  - the canopy/ground partition does not enter the total.
- **Land-only cells:** A − atmosphere = −0.025 (radiation-band vs albedo-band mapping); fsa − atmosphere = −0.093.
- **Why the round-1 figure overstates it.** Round 1's "−0.573 land" compared txl·fsa with txl × the cell-mean net SW. In coastal cells the cell mean includes water's low albedo, so it is not the land's share.
- **SW1 (switch):** sav and sag are rescaled so that fsa = A.
  - Closure: C = A = 39.4202 exactly; land-only fsa − atmosphere = −0.025, the radiation's own band mapping.
  - Impact: land uswrf −0.81 W m-2 mean (rms 12, noise 2.1); tmpsfc land rms 0.96 (noise 0.51); tmp2m land +0.015 K; T rms 0.172.
  - Class: NUMERICAL CONSISTENCY (PHYSICS), beyond noise. The glacier (0.36 W m-2 absorbed) is not included.
- **LW:** all types together, −0.033 W m-2 (radiation-time vs current skin T and emissivity). On the cell-mean basis: land +0.115, ice +0.097, water −0.232; a per-type split needs per-type radiation-time emission, which is not metered.
  - Minimal option (not built): after the surface loop, heat the lowest layer by Σ_type frac·(booked net-up LW) − (up − dn)_radiation-time. The atmosphere then receives the LW the surfaces book, and the surfaces keep emitting at their current T (the RRTMGP Jacobian adjustment does this for Ts). Class: PHYSICS / NUMERICAL CONSISTENCY.

## 9. Frame items

- **Aquifer exchange.** Groundwater ΔHs = +10.2041. Defining the aquifer water's enthalpy at the bottom soil layer's T, the terms are:
  - aquifer exchange +10.2137 (qin, overflow and the watmin refill from the aquifer);
  - subsurface runoff −2.3e-5;
  - watmin moves between layers −0.0071 (internal);
  - unattributed −0.0024.

  So **+10.20 closes to 2.4e-3** as a booked open-boundary enthalpy flux. It is a cold-start transient: 97 W m-2 at step 1, about 1 W m-2 by 24 h.
- **Water moved without its sensible heat (soilwater, +0.29; 1.2 W m-2 per soil column).** This mixes boundary enthalpy at the 273.16 reference (infiltration, ET, drainage) with internal redistribution between layers; I have not separated them.
  - A heat-advection option would remove at most this: ≈ 0.03 K/day over 2 m of soil. Impact not run.
  - Snow meltwater percolates at tfrz, so it has no sensible effect.
- **Glacier +0.0071:** the sensible heat c_i·(T − tfrz) of cold ice exported by the NM9 reset/glacier flow. Book it with the runoff.
- **Constant-hfus Kirchhoff (R_E +0.200, of which 20.7 W m-2 at step 1 from the initial-condition freeze; ≈ 0.01 afterwards).**
  - Noah-MP freezes and melts at constant hfus while its heat capacity changes from c_w to c_i (soil ice 2094 J/kg/K). The per-kg gap is 2094·(T − tfrz) J/kg: 6 % of hfus at −10 °C.
  - Option: L_f(T) = hfus + (c_w − c_i)(T − tfrz) in phasechange.
  - It is a trade-off, as for Thompson's K1: it changes freezing heat by the same few per cent in cold soils.
